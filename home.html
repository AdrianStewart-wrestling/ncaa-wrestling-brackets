About

Tournament Central is a fan-built home for the NCAA Division I Wrestling Championships. Follow the official bracket as results come in, fill out your own bracket and see how your projected team scores stack up, or open any championship since 1999.

Click any wrestler in a bracket to see his path through the tournament, and in NCAA History, his whole NCAA career.

Data & Sources

The official 2026 bracket is entered result by result by Tournament Central operators as bouts finish, and team scores and All-Americans are calculated from those results.

NCAA History replays 27 championships, 1999 through 2026, from published championship results. There is no 2020 entry because that year's championships were canceled.
