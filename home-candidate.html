Documented source decisions

Where sources disagree or a bracket is incomplete, the decision is recorded bout by bout. Nothing is reconciled silently.

    What is recorded for each bout

    Tournament Central preserves the source result rather than silently normalizing it. For historical years with bout-level provenance, the source text, the source itself and any documented decision are retained alongside the reconstructed result. When a source doesn’t print a result, Tournament Central says so rather than guessing.

    The official 2026 bracket

    Entered result by result by Tournament Central operators as bouts finish. Team scores and All-Americans are calculated from those results.

    About

    Tournament Central is a fan-built home for the NCAA Division I Wrestling Championships. Follow the official bracket as results come in, fill out your own bracket and see how your projected team scores stack up, or open any championship since 1980.

    Click any wrestler in a bracket to see his path through the tournament, and in NCAA History, his whole NCAA career.
